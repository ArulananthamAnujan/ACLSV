About the alliance

Language connects us to who we are.

Community languages carry stories, traditions and knowledge between generations. They help young people connect with family, culture and the wider world.

The Alliance of Community Language Schools – Victoria brings a shared vision for supporting the schools, educators and volunteers who make that learning possible.
